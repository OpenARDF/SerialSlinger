# SerialSlinger 2.0.20

## User-visible changes

- Preserve the selected number of scheduled days when changing start, finish, or duration, and show the final schedule with every configured date on Android and desktop.
- With SignalSlinger firmware 2.0.4, show device-recorded session history, including overheating pauses, resumptions, completed sessions, interruptions, and expired windows. Show available stop reasons and transmitter times.
- Distinguish an expired schedule from a confirmed completed session. Unknown stop times, including after an unexpected reset, remain explicitly unavailable.
- Align Android and desktop thermal-protection controls with the firmware's enabled 65 C default.

## Stability and reliability

- Verify the device's Days To Run readback against the user's selection, even if another refresh changes the displayed count while a schedule dialog is open.
- Retain session outcomes and history through connection and refresh, while clearing another transmitter's history when its full identity changes.
- Preserve SignalSlinger clock times when displaying history and matching records to schedules, avoiding timezone shifts. Arducon UTC time conversion remains supported.
- Keep legacy SignalSlinger compatibility and retain explicit interruption reports without inventing successful completion or a stop timestamp.

## Android release notes

Preserve your selected schedule length and see every scheduled date. Schedule writes now verify the selected day count. With SignalSlinger firmware 2.0.4, view session history with stop reasons and device times, including overheating pauses, interruptions, and confirmed completion. Fixed history disappearing after refresh and incorrect timezone shifts. Expired schedules no longer imply successful completion.

## Field testing

Use with SignalSlinger firmware 2.0.4 for device-recorded history. Reconnect after updating, then read back the requested start date, daily finish time, and all three scheduled dates. Keep temperature protection enabled and save the app session logs after the test.
